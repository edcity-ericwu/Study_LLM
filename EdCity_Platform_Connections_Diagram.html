Every box and arrow below is traceable to a story (S1–S9) or a decided flow (Flows A–F). 1 · System connection map

All connections cross deliberately designed async contracts — nothing volatile is ever embedded in the fragile SMS core. EdMarket gates what can appear in a teacher's launcher; the shared taxonomy (red, top right) is the single biggest unbuilt dependency — performance views (S7) and vendor data aggregation (S5) both wait on it.

2 · The vendor-tool lifecycle (stories 1–8)

Story order follows a tool's real lifecycle: vetted → subscribed → discovered and used → output reviewed → visible in trends → reflected to the student. Ms. Ho's roster work (S2) is the foundation everything reads from. The mandated Subject AI Assistant (S9) runs parallel to this whole lifecycle and needs none of it — which is exactly why it can ship first.
